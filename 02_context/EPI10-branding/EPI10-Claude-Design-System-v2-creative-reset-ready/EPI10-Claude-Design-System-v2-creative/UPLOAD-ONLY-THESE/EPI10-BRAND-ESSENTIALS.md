# EPI10 Salud — Brand essentials

## The opportunity

EPI10 is for people who have already moved beyond basic health advice. They train, compare supplements, care about nutrition, monitor sleep or actively invest in their wellbeing. They have already followed general recommendations and now want to understand what may be more relevant to them personally.

The brand should make this feel like entering a new stage of self-knowledge: an invitation to go deeper, with an expert guide rather than alone.

## What EPI10 is

EPI10 Salud is a premium brand of **advanced personalized wellbeing**.

It helps people understand genetic information and turn it into clearer priorities and actions related to wellbeing.

## The initial offer

The working product name is **NutriWell**. The name is provisional.

NutriWell includes:

- A comprehensive genetic test.
- A questionnaire about habits, objectives and context.
- A personalized report.
- A prioritized action plan.
- A professional interpretation session.

The analysis may cover nutrition, metabolism, movement, performance, recovery, sleep, ancestry, genetic traits, pharmacogenetics and hereditary vulnerabilities.

## Where the value lives

EPI10 does not claim that the external laboratory test is exclusive. Its distinctive value is the layer after the test:

- Integrating genetics with habits and context.
- Translating complexity into understandable meaning.
- Separating what matters from what is merely interesting.
- Turning interpretation into prioritized next steps.
- Accompanying the person through the results.

The customer should never feel that they have simply purchased a PDF full of data.

## Audience

The initial audience is the consumer, not healthcare professionals, pharmacies or corporate partners.

The primary customer is already active in fitness and wellness but is not necessarily an elite athlete. They are curious, informed, selective and willing to invest in high-quality self-knowledge.

People motivated by illness or an existing health concern are a secondary audience. The launch should not be framed through fear or disease.

## Positioning

Strategic territory:

> **Advanced personalized wellbeing**

Core idea:

> **Take your wellbeing to the next level by understanding what makes you unique.**

The emotional movement is from generic advice and trial-and-error toward deeper understanding, personal relevance and confident action.

## Message priorities

Lead with:

- Self-knowledge.
- Personal relevance.
- Nutrition, fitness, recovery, sleep and wellbeing.
- Expert interpretation and action.
- The feeling of entering a new level.

Reveal later:

- Ancestry and traits as discovery.
- Pharmacogenetics and hereditary information as sensitive layers.

## Sensitive information

The user has granular control over whether they want to receive sensitive findings.

Wellbeing results can be interpreted by an EPI10 professional. Pharmacogenetic or hereditary findings that require clinical context are reviewed or escalated to an appropriately qualified health professional.

Predisposition is not automatically presented as diagnosis. Pharmacogenetic information is never framed as an instruction to change medication without a clinician.

## Character

The brand is:

- Scientific, not clinical or cold.
- Human, not sentimental.
- Premium, not ostentatious.
- Aspirational, not miraculous.
- Responsible, not timid.
- Contemporary, not futurist for its own sake.

EPI10 behaves as a guide, translator and curator. It does not behave as a genetic oracle, biohacking guru, traditional clinic or aggressive supplement seller.

## Visual intent

The supplied mockup establishes the desired broad territory:

- Premium editorial wellness.
- Human photography with atmosphere and physical presence.
- Deep aquatic blues and luminous cyan balanced by warm pale surfaces and black.
- Confident scale and strong contrast.
- A feeling of discovery, depth and forward movement.

The mockup is not a template. Do not inherit its page structure, product catalogue, button inconsistencies or exact layouts. Interpret its ambition and mood in a more refined, coherent and original system.

The model has full freedom to select typography, grids, proportions, component geometry, motion, image crops, illustration and data-visualization language.

## Logo

The pale monochrome EPI10 Salud logo is the primary consumer-facing mark. It is intended for dark blue, black, cyan-graded or photographic backgrounds.

Do not introduce the legacy multicolor logo into the new consumer system.

## Social commitment

EPI10's commitment to allocate 10% of profits to social impact is a secondary proof of values, not the main reason to buy.

## Guardrails

Do not:

- Call the product an epigenetic test unless epigenetic markers are directly measured.
- Claim diagnosis, treatment, guaranteed outcomes or exclusive laboratory technology.
- Build the identity around illness or fear.
- Present supplements as the current offer.
- Use generic laboratory stock imagery as the main visual language.
- Default to repetitive cards or a dashboard-like SaaS kit.
- Reproduce the reference PDF page-by-page.
