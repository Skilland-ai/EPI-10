# 06A - Healthie UI Email Evidence v1

## Result

- Subtask status: `Verified` for UI inventory, template controls and one real
  transactional email.
- Evidence type: direct UI and received-email inspection reported by Raul on
  2026-07-12.
- No organization-wide email template was modified.
- One-off/free-form email: not located or tested.

## Objective

Identify which client emails Healthie sends, which are editable, how EPI10
branding appears, what technical sender/reply-to is used and what delivery
history is available.

## Email Template Inventory

`Settings > Email Templates` exposed:

1. `Client Invite`
2. `Appointment Confirmed`
3. `Appointment Reminder`
4. `Online Booking`
5. `Package Purchased`
6. `Program Emails`
7. `Reactivation Emails`
8. `Form Completion Request`

The screen states that changes apply to all providers in the organization.
Available controls include a subject selector/field, rich-text editor, dynamic
variables, `Live Preview` and `Save Template`.

## Client Invite Template

The current template is editable and its default body explicitly mentions
Healthie. The observed subject options were:

- `%{PROVIDER_NAME} has invited you to work together on %{BRAND_NAME}`
- `Access your Patient Portal`
- `Complete signup for your patient account`
- `Instructions for your Patient Portal access with %{BRAND_NAME}`
- `Custom`

Observed variables:

- `%{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}`
- `%{CLIENT_LAST_NAME}`
- `%{CLIENT_FULL_NAME}`
- `%{PROVIDER_NAME}`
- `%{BRAND_NAME}`
- `%{ORG_NAME_OR_SELF}`

`Custom` confirms that this account can enter a custom subject through the UI.
The exact plan entitlement was not established.

The current English body describes Healthie as the online portal and refers to
forms, messages, sessions, payments, virtual meetings and goals. It signs with
Raul's provider name. This copy is not suitable as-is for the EPI10 production
journey.

## Form Completion Request Template

The visible template supports rich-text body content and the same observed
dynamic variables. The body was empty. Subject options were:

- `%{PROVIDER_NAME} has asked you to complete a form`
- `You have a form completion request from %{BRAND_NAME}`
- `Custom`

No real form-completion-request email was sent in this subtask.

## Document Shared Email

`Document Shared` was not present in the editable template inventory. A real
email generated by sharing the test final report was inspected.

Observed content:

- subject: `EPI10 Salud has shared a document with you.`;
- EPI10 Salud logo and organization name;
- `New Document` heading;
- personalized greeting;
- `View Document` button;
- Healthie mobile-app references and store buttons;
- legal footer with HIPAA references.

This email is branded but not fully white-labeled. Healthie remains visible in
the sender domain, app references, infrastructure and legal footer.

## Sender And Reply-To

Observed headers:

```text
From: EPI10 Salud <yourprovider@gethealthie.com>
Reply-To: sales@reboot.academy
mailed-by: em1.gethealthie.com
signed-by: gethealthie.com
security: Standard encryption (TLS)
```

Conclusions limited to this email:

- visible sender name uses EPI10 Salud;
- technical sending domain remains Healthie;
- replies currently route to Reboot, not an EPI10 mailbox;
- own-domain EPI10 sending is not active;
- the actual sender observed was not `hello@gethealthie.com`.

## Notification History For Email Audit

`Client Profile > Notification History` showed type/status/date filters and
time windows. Observed email-related records included three document shares and
the original client invitation. All visible records were `DELIVERED`.

The history records event type, document where relevant, time and delivery
status. No `Sent`-only or `Bounced` record was observed. The UI does not prove
that a recipient opened an email.

## Confirmed Capability

- Eight editable-template categories are visible.
- Some bodies and subjects are editable.
- Custom subject is available for invite and form request in this account.
- Dynamic client/provider/brand variables are available.
- EPI10 name and logo appear in transactional emails.
- Event-driven email delivery and delivery-status history work.
- `Document Shared` sends an email but has no editable template visible.

## Production Gates

- Replace English invite/form-request copy with approved Spanish copy.
- Review every template because edits affect all providers.
- Change `Reply-To` from Reboot to the approved EPI10 operational mailbox.
- Decide whether visible Healthie/app/HIPAA references are acceptable.
- Validate white-label commercial/technical requirements for sending from an
  EPI10 domain.
- Confirm which fixed emails cannot be localized or edited.
- Establish DPO/legal approval for health-related transactional email content.
- Determine whether API-controlled email behavior is required.

White-label remains a critical product gate, not a cosmetic enhancement.

## Remaining Unknowns

- Exact plan entitlement behind custom subject and organization reply-to.
- Own-domain DNS/sender setup and deliverability requirements.
- Editable content available within the six templates not opened in detail.
- Final `Live Preview` rendering for invite and form request.
- Ability to remove Healthie mobile-app and HIPAA references.
- API access to send, suppress or audit individual transactional emails.
- One-off/free-form email capability; current documentation says it is not
  available, but it was not independently tested in UI.

## API Discovery Handoff

Raul explicitly requested that the later API discovery test whether it is
possible to:

1. edit or customize more automatic email types than those exposed under
   `Settings > Email Templates`;
2. create new email templates, new automatic email types or new transactional
   email sends beyond the predefined UI events.

These must be investigated as separate API capabilities:

- read/update an existing template;
- create a reusable template;
- trigger a predefined transactional email;
- send a one-off email;
- define a new event/automation that sends email;
- suppress or replace a native Healthie email.

All six remain `Unknown`. Do not infer availability from the UI editor or from
the existence of Healthie transactional emails.

## Decision

Subtask 06A is closed for UI discovery. Do not modify production-facing email
templates until EPI10 approves Spanish copy, reply-to ownership and white-label
direction. Continue Task 06 with secure chat as a separate channel.
